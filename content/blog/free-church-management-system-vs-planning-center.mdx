Every church needs somewhere to keep its people, forms and follow-ups. Two very different ways to do that are to build your own church management system on Google's tools, or to use a ready-made system such as Planning Center. Both are good options for different churches. Here is how to see which suits yours.

## What is Planning Center?

Planning Center is a well-known church management software made up of separate products that work together. On its pricing page you will find People (your church database), Services (planning services and rotas), Check-Ins, Giving, Groups, Registrations, Calendar, Publishing and Church Center, the app and web pages your members use.

Each product is priced on its own, and several have a free tier with limits. When we checked Planning Center's pricing page, for example, People was listed as free with an unlimited database, Check-Ins was free for a small number of check-ins on a Sunday, and Groups was free for a small number of people in groups. Paid plans go up as your church grows, and Giving has processing fees on each donation that differ from country to country.

Prices and limits change, so always check [Planning Center's own pricing page](https://www.planningcenter.com/pricing) for the current details before you decide.

## What is a church management system on Google?

The other option is to build your own system from Google's tools: Google Forms, Sheets, Drive, Contacts and some ready-made scripts. Course 1 of our [How to Make a Website course](/shop/how-to-make-a-website) shows you how, step by step, with Claude, the AI assistant, guiding you.

What you end up with:

- Online forms for visitors, baptism, groups, events and pastoral care
- A People list and a Follow Ups list, so somebody phones each new visitor
- Groups in Google Contacts, with group emails that use each person's name
- Thank-you and birthday emails, and a regular backup of your lists
- A private team dashboard, on a computer or a phone

On a free Gmail account the system has no monthly fees. Google Workspace is optional: it is charged per person, unless Google for Nonprofits accepts your registered charity. You can read the full details on our [church management system page](/church-management-system).

## What does each option cost?

With Planning Center you pay for the products and plan levels you choose, plus giving fees if you use Giving. A small church may fit within the free tiers; a growing church will usually pay more as it grows.

With the Google approach you pay once for the course (Course 1 is US$97, or it comes in the complete package). There is no monthly fee on free Gmail. The course works on Claude's free plan, but we recommend one month of a paid Claude plan while you build. A short web address for the dashboard is optional.

The bigger cost of the Google approach is time. Expect about 5 to 7 hours of hands-on work for Course 1, after a short set-up session for Claude.

## Why would a church choose Planning Center?

There are good reasons, and we want to be fair about them:

- **It is ready-made.** You sign up, set it up and bring your people across. Nobody has to build anything.
- **Check-ins.** Planning Center Check-Ins handles attendance, and families can check their children in. Course 1 does not build a check-in system.
- **Online giving.** Planning Center Giving processes donations. Our Google system does not process giving.
- **Service planning.** Services helps you build service plans and schedule volunteers into their roles.
- **A member app.** Church Center gives your members an app where they can take part in the life of the church.
- **Support.** A dedicated company looks after the software, with help articles, learning videos and a support team.

For a larger church, or one that needs check-ins and giving from day one, a ready-made system can be the simpler choice.

## Why would a church build its own on Google?

- **No monthly fee on free Gmail.** The money can go to your mission instead.
- **Your data stays in your own Google account**: your Drive, your Sheets, your Contacts.
- **You can change it.** Add a form, change an email or rename a list yourself, with Claude's help.
- **It suits small and new churches**, church plants and outreaches, and churches whose members are still in a spreadsheet.

There are limits. Google limits how many people scripts can email in a day (about 100 on free Gmail and about 1,500 on Workspace when the course was written). If several people do the office work, Google Workspace works better than one shared Gmail login. And somebody needs to look after the system each week.

## How do you choose?

Ask a few simple questions:

1. Do we need check-ins or online giving straight away? If yes, look closely at Planning Center or another dedicated system.
2. Is money tight, and is our church small or new? The Google approach can help you keep costs down.
3. Do we have someone happy to build it with Claude and look after it each week?
