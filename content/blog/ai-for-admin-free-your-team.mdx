Every church team knows the feeling. The week fills up with tasks that are necessary but not calling-defining: formatting the bulletin, organising meeting notes, drafting the same routine emails, maintaining spreadsheets, scheduling follow-ups, and responding to questions that come in every month like clockwork. These things have to be done, and someone has to do them. But they are not why anyone answered a calling. AI handles all of them well. Given clear instructions, it produces accurate first drafts, organises incoming information, writes scheduling messages, and flags what genuinely needs a human decision. What once consumed two or three hours of a staff member's morning can now take twenty minutes of review and approval.
