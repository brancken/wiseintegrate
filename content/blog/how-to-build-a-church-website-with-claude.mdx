You can build a fast, modern church website yourself with Claude, the AI assistant made by Anthropic, without a web developer. Claude does most of the work and checks with you at each step. Your website can be online at your own address within the first day or two, and the whole build, including a church office on Google and getting found on Google, takes about three to four days of work.

## What you end up with

A church website built this way can have:

- **Your own web address** with the padlock, the little lock that tells visitors your site is safe.
- **A calm, professional design** in your colours with your logo, and a day and night mode.
- **A website that is also your app.** People save it to their phone's home screen, so there is no need to build a separate app or list it in an app store.
- **Everything a visitor needs**: service times, a map with directions, and events from your calendar with an "add to my calendar" button.
- **Sermons that appear by themselves.** Upload a video to YouTube and your Sermons page shows it.
- **Contact and prayer forms** that send to your inbox, with a human check that keeps spam robots out.
- **Pages you edit yourself** in your browser: words, photos, times and new pages, with no code.

Our sample church site scored 100 out of 100 in all four areas of Google's own speed test on a phone. You can see it at [howtomakeawebsite.fyi](https://howtomakeawebsite.fyi), and a real church using it at [shilohministries.nz](https://shilohministries.nz), Shiloh Ministries in Auckland, New Zealand.

## The steps, in order

### 1. Set up Claude

Install the Claude desktop app, connect your tools safely and learn how to ask Claude and check its work.

### 2. Buy your web address

Your domain is your church's name on the internet, such as yourchurch.org, rented by the year. Cloudflare looks after it and points it to your website.

### 3. Store your website's files on GitHub

GitHub stores your website's files safely and keeps every earlier version.

### 4. Copy the sample church site

You do not start from a blank page. You copy a finished sample church website and make it yours.

### 5. Put it online with Vercel

Vercel keeps your website switched on, day and night. Once it is connected, your site is live.

### 6. Make it yours

Change the colours, logo, words and photos. Add your service times, events, sermons and giving details. If you are not ready with every page, launch with a few and add more later.

### 7. Connect your web address, forms and protection

Point your own address to the site, switch on the contact and prayer forms with the human check, and run the final security checks before you tell people about it.

| Part | Hands-on time |
|---|---|
| Set up Claude | about 1 to 1.5 hours |
| Build your website | about 6 to 8 hours, plus up to an hour of waiting after buying and connecting the domain |
| Your church office on Google (optional) | about 5 to 7 hours |

All three courses together come to about three to four days of work, spread over a week or two because of the waiting. Writing your own words and choosing photos takes as long as you want it to.

## What you need before you start

- A Windows 10 or later PC, or a Mac with macOS 13 or later. 8 GB of memory works; 16 GB is better.
- Steady broadband internet and a smartphone for the 2-step security codes.
- Your logo and a few good photos, if you have them. If not, the sample's pictures do until you have your own.

You do not need a degree or a web design diploma. If you can use email and a web browser, you can do this.

## What Claude does, and what you do

Claude writes your texts, fills in your settings, prepares every file and opens the right websites. Every time you need to look at something, it checks with you before moving on. You only do what Claude must not do for you: type your own passwords and codes, click "Allow" on a permission screen, pay for your domain, and say yes before anything is published.
