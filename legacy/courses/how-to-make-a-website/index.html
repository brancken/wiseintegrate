Good to know

What you need, and what it costs to run.

What you need

  • A Windows 10 or later PC, or a Mac with macOS 13 or later (8 GB memory, 16 recommended)
  • Steady broadband internet and a smartphone
  • Claude: the course works on the free plan; we recommend one month of a paid plan while you build

What it costs to run

  • Your web address: usually about 10 to 15 US dollars a year
  • Claude: one month of a paid plan recommended
  • Hosting: at the time we made this course, free for a church or personal website; a business needs the host's paid plan
  • Everything else the course uses: no charge for what the course uses

How long it takes

  • All three courses: about three to four days of work, spread over a week or two
  • Your website can be online within the first day or two
